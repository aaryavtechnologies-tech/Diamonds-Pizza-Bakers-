WELCOME TO OUR KITCHEN

Authentic Pizzeria & Bakery in Rajgarh

At Diamonds Pizza Bakers, we bring the genuine craft of artisanal baking to the heart of Rajgarh, Churu. Every pizza starts with our slow-fermented, hand-stretched dough, layered with rich Italian tomato sauce, 100% pure dairy mozzarella, and vibrant seasonal toppings.

Beyond pizzas, our kitchen crafts succulent gourmet burgers, authentic street bites, and refreshing chilled beverages — all prepared fresh upon order with unwavering dedication to flavor, hygiene, and honest hospitality.
